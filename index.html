Sipa

Keep the sipa in the air! Tap or click it once it starts falling to kick it back up. Hit its left side to send it right, and its right side to send it left. On a keyboard, press Space when the sipa gets low. If it touches the ground, the game is over.

Luksong Tinik

Jump over the tinik, the stacked hands. They get taller and faster as you go. Tap, click, or press Space or the Up arrow to jump.

Hulaan ang Salita

Guess the Filipino word one letter at a time. You can miss 6 times. Use the buttons below or your keyboard. When the round ends, you'll see what the word means in English.
